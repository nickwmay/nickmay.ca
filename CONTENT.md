## The hook

"My career took the jungle-gym route. Theatre. Hospitality. Tech." The site is a playbill
for that career, with two ways in:

- **Front of House**: the polished program (cream paper, velvet, spotlights).
- **Backstage**: the same show as the crew sees it (prompt book, cue sheet, terminal).

Visitors should leave knowing who Nick is, and how to get in touch.
